Ordering

Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern on a business day leave our Columbus, Ohio warehouse the same afternoon. Anything placed later is packed first thing the next morning.

You can change the size or colour of an item for up to one hour after checkout from the Orders page. After that the parcel is already moving through our packing line and we can no longer intercept it.

We accept all major cards, PayPal and Northwind gift cards. Gift cards never expire and can be combined with a card payment at checkout.

Shipping

Standard delivery takes three to five business days within the contiguous United States and is free on orders over $75. Express delivery arrives in one to two business days for a flat $18.

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and Japan. International customers are responsible for import duties, which our carrier collects at the door.

Every parcel includes a tracking link sent by email. If the tracking page hasn't updated in four days, write to us and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

Returns and exchanges

Changed your mind? Send any unworn item back within 60 days and we will credit the full amount to your original payment method within five business days of it reaching us.

Exchanges for a different size are free in both directions. Print the prepaid label from your account, drop the parcel at any UPS location, and the replacement ships as soon as the return is scanned.

Sale items marked “final” and personalised items with custom embroidery can't be sent back, though they are still covered by the workmanship guarantee below.

Damaged or faulty gear

If a parcel shows up crushed, torn or with something missing, take a photo before opening it fully and send it to support within seven days. We'll dispatch a replacement immediately, no need to wait for the original to come back.

Our workmanship guarantee lasts for the lifetime of the product. Broken zips, failed seams and delaminating soles are repaired or replaced at no cost; normal wear, like faded fabric or worn tread, is not covered.

Product care

Wash waterproof shells cold on a gentle cycle with a technical cleaner, never fabric softener, which clogs the membrane. Tumble dry on low for twenty minutes to reactivate the water-repellent finish.

Leather boots should be brushed clean after every muddy hike and treated with wax twice a season. Let them dry slowly at room temperature; placing them next to a radiator will crack the leather.

Store down sleeping bags loose in the large cotton sack they came with rather than the compression sack, so the insulation keeps its loft for years.

Sizing

Our jackets have an athletic cut. If you plan to wear a thick fleece underneath, or you are between sizes, choose the larger one.

The youth range covers ages 6 to 14, and every piece in it passes CPSIA testing for lead and phthalates. Hoods on youth jackets detach with snaps instead of drawcords, which can catch on playground equipment.

Your account and privacy

We only use your address and order history to fulfil purchases and, if you opt in, to send our seasonal catalogue. We never rent, trade or hand your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

You can download everything we hold about you, or permanently erase your account, from Settings → Privacy. Erasure completes within 30 days, apart from invoices we're legally required to keep.

Sustainability

Since 2023 every jacket uses recycled nylon, and our down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard. Shipping boxes are made from 100% recycled cardboard with paper tape.

Through Northwind Renew, bring back any used Northwind product to a store and get a $20 voucher. Gear in good shape is cleaned and resold; the rest is recycled into insulation.

Our team in Columbus answers chat and email seven days a week from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern. For anything urgent, phone 1-800-555-0142.
